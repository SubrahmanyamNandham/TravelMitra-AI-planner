This Cookie Policy explains how Travel Mitra uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website or use our mobile applications. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve user experience, and provide information to the website owner.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as:

  • Web beacons: Tiny transparent images embedded in pages or emails that signal when content has been viewed.
  • Local storage: Data stored in your browser to remember preferences between sessions.
  • Session storage: Temporary data stored for the duration of a single browsing session.
  • Pixel tags: Used to understand user interaction with our marketing communications.
02

Types of Cookies We Use

We use four categories of cookies on Travel Mitra, each serving a distinct purpose:

Always Active

Essential Cookies

Optional

Optional

03

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Travel Mitra platform to operate. Without them, core features would not function correctly. You cannot opt out of essential cookies.

  • Session management: Keeps you logged in as you navigate between pages.
  • Security tokens: Protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks.
  • Shopping cart / booking state: Preserves your itinerary and booking progress.
  • Load balancing: Ensures requests are distributed efficiently across our servers.
  • Cookie consent: Remembers your cookie preferences.

Duration: Session (deleted when browser closes) or up to 1 year.

04

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors use Travel Mitra — which pages are visited most, how users navigate, and whether any errors occur. This data helps us improve our platform.

  • Google Analytics: Page views, session duration, and user flow analysis.
  • Hotjar: Heatmaps and session recordings to understand user behaviour.
  • Internal analytics: Search query patterns and feature usage metrics.

All analytics data is aggregated and anonymised. No personally identifiable information is stored in these cookies.

Duration: Up to 2 years.

05

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow our platform to remember your preferences and tailor your experience accordingly:

  • Your preferred language and currency settings.
  • Your home city or departure airport for faster search.
  • Recently viewed destinations and hotels.
  • Accessibility preferences such as font size or contrast settings.
  • Whether you have dismissed certain banners or notifications.

Disabling these cookies may mean that certain personalisation features will not work as expected.

Duration: Up to 1 year.

06

Marketing & Advertising Cookies

We use marketing cookies to show you relevant travel advertisements on Travel Mitra and partner websites. These cookies track your browsing activity to build a profile of your travel interests.

  • Retargeting: Show you ads for destinations or hotels you previously viewed.
  • Attribution: Measure which marketing channels drive bookings.
  • Social media pixels: Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags for campaign optimisation.

You can opt out of marketing cookies at any time via our cookie consent banner without affecting your ability to use the platform.

Duration: Up to 1 year.

07

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. Third parties that may set cookies include:

  • Google: Analytics and advertising services.
  • Meta (Facebook): Social sharing and advertising.
  • Razorpay / PayU: Payment processing.
  • Intercom: Customer support chat widget.
  • YouTube: Embedded travel video content.

Please review the respective privacy policies of these providers for information about their cookie practices.

08

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit Travel Mitra, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept all cookies, reject optional cookies, or customise your preferences by category.

Browser Settings

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete stored cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Receive a notification before a cookie is stored.
  • Block all cookies (note: this will impact platform functionality).

Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions: Chrome, Firefox, Safari.

Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out: optout.aboutads.info
