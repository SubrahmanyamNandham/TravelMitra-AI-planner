At Travel Mitra, your privacy matters deeply. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform and services. Please read it carefully.

01

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account details: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Travel preferences: Destination preferences, travel dates, group size, and budget range.
  • Payment information: Billing address and payment method details (processed securely via third-party payment processors).
  • Profile information: Profile photo, travel history, and any other details you add to your profile.
  • Communications: Messages you send to us via support, feedback forms, or email.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, search queries, and time spent on the platform.
  • Device information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Location data: Approximate location based on IP address (and precise location if you grant permission).
  • Cookies & similar technologies: As described in our Cookie Policy.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to provide, improve, and personalise our travel services:

  • To create and manage your Travel Mitra account.
  • To process bookings, itineraries, and payment transactions.
  • To personalise travel recommendations and destination suggestions.
  • To send booking confirmations, trip reminders, and service updates.
  • To respond to your customer support inquiries.
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve our platform experience.
  • To send promotional offers and travel inspiration (with your consent).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud or security threats.
03

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only in the following circumstances:

  • Travel partners: Hotels, airlines, tour operators, and other travel service providers necessary to fulfil your bookings.
  • Service providers: Third-party vendors who assist us (payment processing, email delivery, analytics, cloud hosting).
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.
  • With your consent: Any other sharing we will ask for your explicit permission first.

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

04

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on Travel Mitra. This includes session cookies, preference cookies, and analytics tools. For full details, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

05

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide services and comply with our legal obligations. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained until you request deletion or for up to 3 years after account inactivity.
  • Booking and transaction records are retained for 7 years for financial and legal compliance.
  • Marketing preferences are retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.
  • Log and usage data is retained for up to 12 months.
06

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@travelmitra.io.

07

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted to and from our platform.
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive data including payment information.
  • Access controls ensuring only authorised personnel can access user data.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and keep your account credentials confidential.

08

Children's Privacy

Travel Mitra is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us personal information should contact us immediately at privacy@travelmitra.io.

09

Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites, travel partners, or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit through links on Travel Mitra.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting a prominent notice on our website.
  • Sending an email notification to your registered address.
  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

Your continued use of Travel Mitra after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
